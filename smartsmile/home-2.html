Clinical Decision Guide

BRACES VS. CLEAR ALIGNERS COMPARISON.

Compare the biomechanical and lifestyle factors of modern fixed braces versus removable clear aligners to understand which aligns with your lifestyle.

Evaluation Metric Traditional & Ceramic Braces Clear Aligners
Aesthetics & Discretion Visible brackets (metal) or subtle tooth-toned ceramic. Noticeable up close. Virtually Invisible transparent medical-grade polymers.
Removability & Eating Fixed 24/7. Avoid sticky, very hard, or crunchy foods to protect brackets. Fully Removable during meals. No dietary restrictions whatsoever.
Oral Hygiene Routine Requires orthodontic floss threaders and interdental brushes around wires. Standard Flossing & Brushing. Simple aligner cleaning rinse.
Biomechanical Range Ideal for All Complexities: Severe rotations, skeletal bites, surgical cases. Excellent for mild to moderately severe crowding, gaps, and overbites.
Clinic Appointment Cadence Adjustment every 4 to 6 weeks for archwire adjustments. Check-ins every 8 to 10 weeks; progress trackable via patient dashboard.
Patient Compliance Factor Zero compliance worries; appliances work non-stop around the clock. Requires disciplined wear for 20–22 hours daily for optimal velocity.
*Disclaimer: Final treatment appropriateness is diagnosed individually during your SmartSmile comprehensive examination.

Biological Orthodontics

THE SCIENCE OF PHYSIOLOGICAL TOOTH MOVEMENT.

Teeth do not move through brute force—they move through biological cellular remodeling. Gentle continuous pressure activates microscopic cells that safely reshape bone.

Osteoclasts (Pressure Side)

Resorb bone cells gently where tooth pressure is applied, creating space for controlled movement.

Osteoblasts (Tension Side)

Deposit fresh mineralized bone behind the moving root, locking the tooth into permanent stability.
