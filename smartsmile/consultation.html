Common Questions

CONSULTATION FAQS.

The initial consultation is dedicated to diagnostics, 3D scanning, and clinical planning. If you are excited to begin immediately, we can often schedule bonding or take final digital aligner impressions during the same appointment block.

No referral is required! You can book directly with our orthodontic clinic anytime. However, we recommend having regular cleanings and checkups with your family dentist prior to beginning orthodontic therapy.

Our initial orthodontic consultation is completely complimentary for new patients, including the 3D optical scan and orthodontic outcome preview. There is zero obligation to proceed.
