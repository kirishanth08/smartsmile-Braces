WHAT DOES BOARD CERTIFICATION MEAN?

Only 1 in 3 orthodontists achieves the rigorous distinction of American Board of Orthodontics (ABO) certification. It requires hundreds of additional hours demonstrating verified clinical case outcomes before an examining board.

Postdoctoral Specialization:
3 additional full-time years of university orthodontic hospital residency after dental school.
Peer-Reviewed Case Standards:
Rigorous clinical examination testing both functional occlusion balance and facial aesthetic harmony.
