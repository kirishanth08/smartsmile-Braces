Case Spotlight #104

SOPHIA'S SMILE CONFIDENCE JOURNEY.

Sophia, a 26-year-old marketing manager, had been self-conscious about her front teeth overlapping and a 5mm protrusion since high school. Traditional metal braces were not an appealing option due to weekly public presentations.

“Dr. Carter showed me the 3D projection on the scanner screen during my first visit. Seeing how my teeth would look 12 months ahead gave me complete peace of mind. Wearing the trays was effortless, and my clients never even realized I had them in.”

Chief Complaint
Crowding & Overjet
Appliance
Clear Aligners
Active Duration
14 Months
Chronological Progress

THE 14-MONTH MILESTONE PROGRESSION.

How Sophia’s dental alignment progressed at each clinical check-in.

M0

Baseline Setup & Attachments

High-resolution 3D scan, tray sequence delivered, attachments placed on premolars to enable rotation.

M4

Arch Expansion

Posterior teeth gently expanded outward, creating room for upper incisors to derotate without extraction.

M8

Overjet Reduction

Upper front teeth retracted smoothly into harmony with the lower arch. Facial profile visibly improved.

M11

Refinement Aligners

6 micro-detailing trays used to perfect interdental contact tightness and smile arc symmetry.

M14

Debonding & Clear Retainers

Smart attachments polished away, enamel remineralized, and custom nighttime retainers delivered.

NOW

Lifelong Smiling Stability

Sophia continues wearing her nighttime retainers with zero dental shift detected at her 1-year follow-up.

Clinical Commentary

Dr. Olivia Carter, DDS, MS • Board-Certified Orthodontist

“Sophia’s case represents an ideal example of non-extraction arch remodeling in young adults. By leveraging sequential distalization and slight buccal expansion, we generated 4.8mm of arch perimeter without stripping healthy enamel. Her compliance with wearing the aligners 21+ hours daily enabled us to complete her case two months ahead of our original estimate.”

Key Clinical Takeaway: Patient compliance and digital movement calibration together produce reliable, predictable smile aesthetics without invasive surgery.
