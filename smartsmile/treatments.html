Clinical Precision

ADVANCED ORTHODONTIC DIAGNOSTICS & TECH.

We combine cutting-edge intraoral imaging with digital biomechanics to optimize tooth movement safety and speed.

iTero Lumina 3D Scans

Captures 6,000 images per second to create a millimeter-accurate photorealistic 3D replica of your mouth in under 90 seconds without messy gooey putty trays.

Ultra-Low-Dose CBCT

Provides comprehensive 3D volumetric views of your roots, alveolar bone density, and airway corridors with up to 80% lower radiation than legacy equipment.

Digital Indirect Bonding

Our orthodontists pre-position brackets digitally on a 3D model, placing all brackets at once with high-precision custom transfer trays in half the appointment time.

WHICH ORTHODONTIC SOLUTION FITS YOU?

Compare treatment profiles based on common anatomical concerns and lifestyle needs.

Mild Spacing or Relapse

Previous braces patients experiencing minor shift or front tooth crowding.

Recommended: Clear Aligners Express

Working Professionals

Adults seeking discretion in meetings and presentations without dietary limits.

Recommended: Clear Aligners Full

Active Teenagers

Active school schedules, musical instruments, and high school sports.

Recommended: Ceramic or Metal Braces

Complex Skeletal Bite

Severe overjet, underbites, crossbites, or impacted canine repositioning.

Recommended: Fixed Precision Braces
