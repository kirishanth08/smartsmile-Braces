WHAT HAPPENS AT YOUR FIRST VISIT.

Your initial consultation takes approximately 45 minutes and is 100% painless. Here is our step-by-step visit protocol:

01

Warm Welcome & Lounge

Enjoy complimentary sparkling water or tea while checking in digitally with our concierge team.

02

3D Optical Scan & Photos

Our technologist captures a 3D digital model of your teeth in under 3 minutes with zero goopy molds.

03

Doctor Exam & Simulation

Your orthodontist examines your bite and shows your 3D tooth movement simulation on screen.

04

Clear Proposal & 0% Plans

Review your customized all-inclusive fee, insurance deductions, and flexible monthly payment options.
