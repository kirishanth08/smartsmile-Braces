Patient Spotlight

MARCUS'S CONFIDENT SMILE JOURNEY.

Marcus, a 23-year-old graphic designer, felt that the 4mm spacing between his central incisors drew unwanted attention and made him hesitant to smile in studio photography.

“I wanted the structural strength and speed of fixed braces, but couldn’t imagine having shiny silver metal on my teeth. Dr. Bennett recommended tooth-colored ceramic brackets. They blended so naturally with my enamel that friends barely noticed them across a table.”

Biomechanical Execution:
  • Low-force continuous elastomeric powerchains utilized to draw the central incisors together smoothly.
  • Progressive rectangular archwires calibrated to prevent tipping and achieve parallel bodily root translation.
  • Fixed bonded lingual retainer placed from canine to canine to guarantee permanent space closure.
Sequential Timeline

THE 12-MONTH MILESTONE BREAKDOWN.

Marcus’s orthodontic progress monitored across every clinical activation visit.

M0

Ceramic Bracket Bonding

Tooth-colored translucent ceramic brackets placed with aesthetic coated initial archwires engaged.

M3

Rotational Correction

Rotated lateral incisors fully aligned and leveled into the primary dental archform.

M6

Central Space Closure

Elastomeric chain mechanics engaged; 4mm midline gap fully closed with facial midline synchronized.

M9

Bodily Root Paralleling

Rigid stainless steel wire engaged to torque the incisor roots parallel, eliminating relapse tendency.

M12

Debonding & Retention

Ceramic brackets gently removed, adhesive cleaned, and permanent lingual retainer bonded seamlessly.

NOW

Lifelong Gap-Free Smile

Marcus enjoys absolute stability and full confidence in his photographic and professional pursuits.

Clinical Commentary

Dr. Marcus Bennett, DDS, MS • Senior Orthodontic Specialist

“Midline diastemas carry a high natural rate of relapse if root torque is not meticulously maintained during closure. With Marcus, our ceramic system allowed us to exert 3D control through full-dimension finishing archwires while preserving the aesthetic discretion he needed. A bonded lingual multistrand wire locks his contact point permanently.”

Key Clinical Takeaway: Bodily root uprighting combined with permanent lingual retention prevents diastema reopening throughout adulthood.
