Founded on Innovation

BUILT BY ORTHODONTISTS FOR REAL LIVES.

Founded in 2014 by Dr. Olivia Carter, SmartSmile emerged from a frustration with outdated orthodontic practices: cold waiting rooms, uncomfortable goopy impression trays, and vague treatment timelines that left patients wondering when their braces would finally come off.

We invested in 3D digital intraoral scanning, high-resolution movement simulations, and custom aligner fabrication. By turning orthodontics into a collaborative digital experience, our patients see every milestone before their treatment even begins.

“We don’t just straighten teeth. We restore confidence, improve airway function, and give patients a smile that feels authentically theirs.”
— Dr. Olivia Carter, Founder
Guiding Clinical Principles

OUR ORTHODONTIC PHILOSOPHY.

Rooted in biological dental movement, airway health, and long-term functional stability rather than quick cosmetic shortcuts.

Gentle Biological Movement

Teeth respond best to light, continuous physiological forces. Our treatment plans respect periodontal ligament biology to protect roots and maximize comfort.

Facial Symmetry & Aesthetics

We look at the complete facial profile: lip support, smile arc curvature, and cheekbone harmony, ensuring your smile flatters your unique face.

Non-Extraction First Approach

Whenever clinically safe, we prioritize gentle arch expansion and interproximal space creation to preserve your full natural dentition and broad smile aesthetics.

Advanced Technology Suite

THE DIGITAL SUITE BEHIND YOUR SMILE.

Precision orthodontics requires advanced diagnostics. We have eliminated guesswork through next-generation imaging tools:

  • 3D Optical Intraoral Scanners: High-speed optical sensors capture photorealistic digital dental models in under 3 minutes.
  • Ultra Low-Dose Digital Cephalometrics: Safe, minimal-radiation imaging to evaluate airway width, jaw bone density, and root parallelism.
  • Direct In-House 3D Printing: Rapid prototyping of retainers and aligner models ensures same-day replacement if appliances are misplaced.
