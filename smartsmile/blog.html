Everyday Wisdom

QUICK ORTHO TIPS FROM OUR CLINIC.

The "Case It or Lose It" Rule

Never wrap aligners or retainers in paper napkins at restaurants—they almost always end up in the trash!

Warm Saltwater Rinses

Experience initial bracket soreness? Rinse with warm salt water for 30 seconds to soothe oral tissues quickly.

Track Your Wear Hours

Wear aligners 22 hours daily. Even small lapses of 3–4 hours can slow orthodontic bone remodeling.
