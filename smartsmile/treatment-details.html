Biomechanics & Technology

HOW CLEAR ALIGNERS WORK.

Engineered using multi-layer polyurethane film designed to exert sustained, controlled physiological pressure without bracket wire irritation.

1. 3D Digital Intraoral Scan

We capture an optical 3D model of your dentition. Our CAD software calculates tooth movements down to 0.1mm increments.

2. Sequential Movement Stages

Each tray advances your teeth by approximately 0.25mm. You swap to a new set every 7–10 days as prescribed by your doctor.

3. Tooth-Colored Smart Attachments

Discreet enamel-matching buttons temporarily bonded to specific teeth ensure complex rotational forces and root leveling.

Why Patients Love Aligners

THE CLEAR ADVANTAGES.

Clear aligners fit seamlessly into modern life, careers, social activities, and athletic routines.

Aesthetic Invisibility

Virtually imperceptible in person and on video calls. Smile with full confidence throughout treatment.

Got Questions?

CLEAR ALIGNERS FAQS.

Answers to the most common questions regarding daily wear, comfort, and aligner care.

For optimal movement and on-time results, aligners should be worn for 20 to 22 hours each day. They should only be removed for meals, drinking hot beverages, and brushing or flossing your teeth.

Most patients report a mild feeling of snug pressure for the first 24–48 hours after inserting a brand-new tray. This is a normal indicator that the teeth are moving gently. Unlike metal brackets, there are no wires to poke your cheeks or lips.

Gently brush your trays with lukewarm water and a soft-bristled toothbrush morning and night. Avoid using hot water as it can warp the medical-grade polymer. Specialized cleaning crystals or aligner foam can also be used.

Don't worry! Contact SmartSmile immediately or message us via your Patient Portal. Depending on where you are in that stage, your orthodontist may advise you to move forward to the next tray early or wear your previous tray while an in-house replacement is 3D printed.
