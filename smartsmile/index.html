COMMON ORTHODONTIC CONDITIONS WE TREAT.

Identify your dental alignment concern below. Our orthodontic specialists create tailored biometric paths using clear aligners and precision brackets to correct complex bite anomalies.

Most Common
Alignment

Dental Crowding

Insufficient jaw space causes teeth to overlap, twist, or rotate, creating plaque traps that increase cavity risk and uneven enamel wear.

Best Path: Clear Aligners
Avg. Duration: 12–16 Months
Explore Solution
Bite Issue
Vertical Overlap

Deep Overbite

Upper front teeth excessively cover lower teeth, sometimes biting into palatal gum tissue and causing chronic strain on the jaw joints.

Best Path: Ceramic Braces
Avg. Duration: 14–20 Months
Explore Solution
Skeletal Class III
Jaw Discrepancy

Underbite & Crossbite

Lower teeth protrude in front of upper teeth, impairing chewing efficiency, compromising facial balance, and causing speech articulation strain.

Best Path: Fixed Braces
Avg. Duration: 16–22 Months
Explore Solution
Aesthetic & Gap
Diastema / Gaps

Spacing & Open Bite

Unwanted gaps between teeth or vertical gaps where front teeth fail to meet upon closing, causing tongue posture imbalance and wear.

Best Path: Clear Aligners
Avg. Duration: 9–14 Months
Explore Solution
